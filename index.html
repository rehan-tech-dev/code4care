Telemetry Categories

Two Pillars of Human Vitality

Complete wellness requires synchronizing biomechanics with neuro-cognitive balance. Code4Care organizes all tools into two dedicated telemetry streams.

Stream 01

Physical Health

Clinical diagnostic equations, cardiovascular zone planning, metabolic telemetry, and hydration targets.

  • BMI & Body Composition — WHO categorizations and healthy weight limits
  • Calorie & BMR Calculator — Mifflin-St Jeor metabolic expenditure
  • Blood Pressure Checker — Systolic/diastolic clinical stage evaluator
  • Heart-Rate Zones — 5 aerobic & anaerobic training boundaries
  • Hydration, Ideal Weight & Pulse Counter — Quick vital taps

Stream 02

Mental Wellness

Autonomic regulation, vagal nerve pacing, stress index scoring, and sleep architecture tracking.

  • Breathing Pacer — Box breathing (4-4-4-4), 4-7-8, and Coherence rhythms
  • Stress Check Index — Validated Perceived Stress Scale evaluation
  • Mood Matrix Tracker — Valence & energy emotional state logging
  • Sleep Log Architecture — 90-minute sleep cycles and sleep debt
  • Meditation Session Timer — Ambient focus with gentle intervals
